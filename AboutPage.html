THE SYSTEM

How Matching Works

A science-backed approach to finding your people

1

You Answer 15 Questions

A mix of multiple choice and text answers covering your interests, career goals, study habits, and collaboration style.

2

Algorithm Compares Profiles

MCQ answers are directly matched. Text answers are broken into keywords and compared for semantic overlap.

3

Similarity Score Calculated

MCQ matches (70% weight) + keyword matches (30% weight) = your final compatibility percentage.
